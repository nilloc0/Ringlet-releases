# Ringlet 3.6

A quick fix-up for 3.5.

## Fixes

- **No more dark square around the lock screen ring.** Its shadow is now soft and follows the ring itself, and the green charging glow isn't cut off at the edges anymore.
- **The lock screen status fits.** At your Charge Limit it reads "Charge limit reached" instead of getting cut off, and the greyed-out Charge to Full Now button is easier to see on light wallpapers.
- **The lock screen ring sits a little lower**, so it doesn't crowd the clock.

## New

- **What's New.** After an update, Ringlet shows you what changed, like this window. You can open it again any time from Settings > General > Help.

## New in 3.5, if you skipped it

- **Ringlet on your lock screen (beta):** your ring, battery, accessories, how long you've been away, Charge to Full Now and music controls, on every display. Turn it on in Settings > General > Lock Screen.
- **Charge to Full Now respects your Charge Limit**, and greys out once you're there.
- **OLED Care**, **Liquid Glass** and sharper unfolded icons.

The lock screen is still in beta. If something looks off, open an issue at [github.com/nilloc0/ringlet-releases/issues](https://github.com/nilloc0/ringlet-releases/issues) or comment on one that's already there. A photo of your screen and the report from Settings > License > Copy Report help a lot.

## How to update

On Ringlet 2.2 or newer: click **Install** in the update banner or in Settings > License. Ringlet downloads the update, checks that it's genuine, and restarts itself.

On an older version: download **Ringlet-3.6.dmg** below, open it, and drag Ringlet to Applications.

**Heads up:** after updating, macOS may ask again for Bluetooth, Location and Focus access. Click Allow and you're set.

Requires macOS 14 or later on Apple Silicon.
