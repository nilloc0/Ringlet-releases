Ringlet
=======

Battery, Wi-Fi, sound and system stats in one menu bar ring.

- Click the ring to open the panel. Click a row to dig in: Wi-Fi networks,
  Bluetooth devices, sound output, battery health, network, CPU, memory, disk.
- Right click for quick controls: volume, brightness, profiles, mute.
- The ring plays short animations ("moments") when you plug in, mute, lose the
  network, connect AirPods or turn on a Focus.
- Settings (right click > Settings) is where you set up your profiles, the
  lock screen, Liquid Glass tint and moments.


INSTALLING
----------

1. Drag Ringlet to the Applications folder in this window.

2. Ringlet isn't notarized by Apple yet, so the first time, macOS will refuse
   to open it. Open Terminal (Applications > Utilities), paste this line and
   press return:

       xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Ringlet.app

   That's the only time you need Terminal. Updates install from inside Ringlet.

3. Open Ringlet from Applications. A short welcome walks you through the rest.

Updating from an older version? Just click Install when Ringlet offers the
update. Coming from DuoBar? Quit it, drag it to the Trash, then install
Ringlet. Your license and settings carry over.


PERMISSIONS
-----------

macOS may ask for Bluetooth (your devices and their batteries), Location (only
so macOS will show your Wi-Fi network's name), Focus (to know when one is on)
and control of Music or Spotify (for the lock screen player). Ringlet does
nothing else with them. After an update, macOS may ask again: click Allow.


YOUR LICENSE KEY
----------------

Settings > License > Manage License, paste your key, click Activate. Without a
key you get a 14 day trial with everything working.


IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
-----------------------

Settings > License > Copy Report, then send it over. It has the log and any
crash details.
